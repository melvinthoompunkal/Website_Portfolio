God View

Global events, markets, and weather each move on their own clock. Watching them meant polling dozens of APIs and hoping nothing slipped between refreshes.

A real-time monitoring platform built on FastAPI, Kafka, and Redis. Concurrent streams from GDELT, financial feeds, and weather APIs are normalized by asynchronous worker pipelines and pushed to the browser over WebSockets, where a React/WebGL frontend renders satellite imagery and GeoJSON overlays. A spatial PostGIS layer accelerates geographic lookups, and a Pandas/Scikit-learn pipeline classifies trends as they arrive.
